A little about me

Making things sound the way they feel.

I'm Eli — an audio production student interested in the ways sound supports music, motion, and storytelling. My work spans recording, editing, sound design, and original music production.

I primarily work in Pro Tools, building projects from raw recordings through finished arrangements. I enjoy both the technical details of editing and the creative challenge of making a scene or song come alive.
